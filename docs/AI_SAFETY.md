# MedKit AI — Clinical AI Safety & Boundary Framework
**SIH Problem Statement: SIH26047 — Patient Case-Taking Software**

---

## 1. Core Clinical Safety Mandate

> **"MedKit AI turns a patient's voice, answers, and medical documents into a verified, structured clinical history—before the doctor begins the consultation."**

MedKit AI is **strictly a physician copilot**, not an autonomous diagnostic system. The software:
1. **NEVER** outputs text claiming "AI diagnosed X" or "Prescribed treatment: Y".
2. **NEVER** executes autonomous clinical actions without attending physician sign-off.
3. **ALWAYS** labels AI extractions and draft summaries as candidate information requiring clinician review.

---

## 2. Standardized Non-Diagnostic Copy Matrix

Every surface of MedKit AI enforces standardized clinical copy:

| Component | Standardized Clinical Copy | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red Flag Detection** | *"Potential red flag detected — immediate clinical assessment recommended."* | Alerts clinician to life-threatening indicators without diagnosing the underlying condition. |
| **Intake Summaries** | *"AI-assisted summary — clinician review required."* | Clarifies that the text is an aggregated narrative synthesized for physician review. |
| **Document OCR** | *"Extracted from uploaded document — verify before use."* | Flags extracted medication names and dosages as candidate entries until clinician verifies. |
| **Interoperability** | *"FHIR-compatible representation / ABDM integration-ready architecture."* | Accurately describes standard data exchange readiness. |

## 3. Deterministic Red Flags Engine

Life-critical alerts are governed by a **deterministic rules engine (`src/features/red-flags/rules-engine.ts`)**, ensuring that critical clinical safety rules are never subject to LLM hallucinations, non-deterministic sampling, or token budget limits:

1. **Cardiovascular / Acute Coronary Syndrome**:
   - Symptoms: Crushing chest pain, radiation to left arm/jaw, diaphoresis, dyspnea.
   - Action: Immediate triage flag; priority queuing.
2. **Respiratory Distress**:
   - Symptoms: Acute shortness of breath, stridor, wheezing with cyanosis.
   - Action: Severe respiratory warning.
3. **Neurological / Acute Stroke**:
   - Symptoms: Sudden facial drooping, unilateral weakness, slurred speech.
   - Action: Time-critical neuro alert.
4. **Sepsis / Hemodynamic Instability**:
   - Symptoms: High fever with altered mental status or extreme hypotension.
   - Action: High priority clinical assessment recommendation.

### Acknowledgment Protocol
- Red flags cannot be dismissed by reception or intake staff.
- Only an authenticated **doctor** or **clinician** can sign off and acknowledge a red flag alert.
- Every acknowledgment records the clinician's full name, timestamp, and an immutable entry in the audit trail.

---

## 4. End-to-End Clinical Provenance Tracking

Every clinical field in a case sheet maintains an explicit `provenance` record:
- `"patient"`: Directly input by the patient or through verbal kiosk answers.
- `"ocr"`: Digitally extracted from an uploaded prescription or diagnostic lab report.
- `"ai"`: Generated via AI copilot synthesis.
- `"clinician"`: Entered or verified by the attending physician.
- `"system/rule"`: Generated by the deterministic rules engine.

---

## 5. Clinician-in-the-Loop Oversight

- All draft cases remain editable by clinicians until explicit **Finalization**.
- Once finalized, the case status changes to `"final"` and becomes immutable, ensuring medical-legal record integrity.
